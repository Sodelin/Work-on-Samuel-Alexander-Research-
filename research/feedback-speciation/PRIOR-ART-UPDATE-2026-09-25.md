The candidate contribution is the **exact comparison in the explicitly specified finite offspring-sampling model and its relationship to the deterministic comparison**. Its priority is unestablished. The finite mathematical result is useful as a reproducible example and a test of extrapolation between models; it does not establish that finite populations generally strengthen epigenetic barriers.

The earlier [prior-work note](finite-epigenetic/PRIOR-WORK.md) already credited finite epigenetic models and normalized fixation. This update adds the explicit migration-before-induction page evidence.

## Corrected biological interpretation

Zhang et al. (2023), [Horticulture Research 10:uhad156](https://doi.org/10.1093/hr/uhad156), **did measure persistent AS1/AS3 phenotypes after transfer to a common garden**. It is incorrect to describe phenotype persistence as unmeasured. Continued warm propagation and the absence of warm AS1/AS2 methylomes leave molecular inheritance versus repeated induction unresolved in the audited design.

No dataset checked in this evidence programme has yet identified the particular migrant-selection-sexual-reset ordering used in our finite model. That is a limit of this checked source set, not a claim that no such dataset exists.
