## Source questions and domain

[Alexander, *Biologically Unavoidable Sequences*, Electronic Journal of Combinatorics 20(1), P31 (2013), DOI 10.37236/3035](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/view/v20i1p31) is the primary source. The **published PDF, Section 6, page 12**, asks for universal populations avoiding a specified avoidable sequence, by analogy with Cherlin–Shelah, and for a characterization of populations realizing a sequence using ordinals analogous to Schmidt/Halin. It specifies neither an embedding category nor a required rank spectrum. [Published text](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/download/v20i1p31/pdf/).

The question concerns directed, edge-labelled genealogies with real birthdates, finite pasts, finitely many roots, finite children, infinitely many vertices and an incoming parent of every label at each nonroot. It is a graph-theoretic problem. Molecular, clinical or protein databases cannot establish these universal quantifiers. Biological literature becomes relevant when changing what an edge or label represents.

Version caution: the published article has 13 pages and its positive eventual-periodicity theorem is Theorem 10. The [arXiv v2 text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1212.0186v2) has 9 pages and numbers that result Theorem 6. The arXiv record dates are 2 December 2012 and 5 February 2013; a later date printed inside a regenerated PDF is not a new publication date.

The new local [TerminalCloneAvoiders.lean](../embedding/TerminalCloneAvoiders.lean) now closes the **exactly one parent of each label** restriction for the nonuniversality construction, starting from an arbitrary supplied avoiding population. First, selecting one parent per label deletes edges, preserves the actual root set and yields inclusion of the selected population's infinite-word language in the original language. Then terminal cloning preserves the selected population's complete infinite-word language and exactly one parent per label while supplying the growth obstruction. The resulting avoider excludes every member of a prescribed countable family of population hosts under injective edge-preserving maps with any finite global stretch bound. ## Mathematical assessment and remaining structure

The following points are deductions or research proposals, not claims quoted from the sources.

1. **The unrestricted embedding result needs its population lemma.** Nonexistence of a universal graph for a large class says nothing automatically about a subclass. Here finite-fibre enlargement is the crucial closure operation: it creates local growth while retaining the allowed path language and the birth/population conditions. This is why the population-specific proof matters even though diagonal growth obstructions are classical.

2. **A prescribed child cap remains a meaningful new category.** At the older public checkpoint, complete finite-fibre enlargement could multiply both parent and child counts. The dated task delta above now supplies a construction preserving exactly one parent per label, so that restriction alone is no longer an unresolved item in this task. A uniform offspring cap still requires a different argument; terminal cloning can increase the number of children. A graph-theoretic host provided by a general theorem also has to be an eligible avoider itself.

3. **The standard rank already answers a precise realization question.** The state must retain the target position: an edge consumes one particular letter, so forgetting phase changes the property. In an avoiding, finitely branching population every fixed-start matching tree is finite. All finite target prefixes nevertheless occur somewhere, because sufficiently late vertices admit the required finite backward parent choices. Therefore the tree joining all starts under a superroot has ordinary rank exactly omega. Its Schmidt rank is 1: deletion of the superroot leaves finite components. The original population itself has directed rays of other label sequences and must not be called rayless merely because it avoids one word.

4. **A rank distinguishing different avoiders needs extra structure.** Height omega and Schmidt rank 1 are constant across these avoiding history trees. A stronger program could study the least natural certificate as a profile over reachable vertex/phase states, its behavior under embeddings, or a finite-separator decomposition that retains labels and phases. This is a proposed research direction, not an unfulfilled premise of Alexander's broad wording.

5. **Biological interpretation should select the category before proving more.** Immediate parenthood, ancestry after suppressing unsampled generations, and locus-specific genetic inheritance produce different maps and path languages. Arbitrarily stretched or ancestry-only embeddings may be natural for a coarse observation model, but they are not the same category as immediate-edge embeddings. No molecular mechanism or empirical species conclusion follows from the abstract ordinal certificate. The existing [biological model scope note](https://github.com/Sodelin/Work-on-Samuel-Alexander-Research-/blob/01db8c4cbb82000950ff7d3e7252b5438416c7c3/BIOLOGICAL-MODEL-SCOPE.md) is an appropriate starting point for that choice.
