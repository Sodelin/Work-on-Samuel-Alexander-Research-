## What this changes in the work plan

The papers provide a related research agenda, but the phrase *open problem* covers several different kinds of work. The RSOS paper provides our first explicit question inventory. The new papers offer potential concrete systems for testing proposed mathematics once their mechanisms are specified.

The initial proof effort therefore targets exact information-preserving abstraction and the memory required by a particular merge/split operator. Empirical calibration questions, model-design proposals and metaphysical claims remain separately labeled. This makes a partial mathematical answer reviewable without representing it as an answer to every surrounding claim.

Additional reader locators: Offloading pp.19–21 asks about shared meaning and environmental pliability; its universal offloading claim is presented as a proposed theoretical conclusion, not an open conjecture. Lifetime imaging p.13 discusses roles and comparisons across signal time scales. These additions do not change the initial proof priorities.
