## Mathematical and scope findings

**Actionwise factorization.** The reused `ExactAbstraction.exact_factor_iff` statement has the necessary hypotheses: a surjective observation and fibre compatibility for every action and pair of detailed states. `exact_factor_unique` establishes uniqueness on the entire reduced codomain only under surjectivity. The bridge correctly restricts a nonsurjective observation to its image and gives a valid unused-state counterexample. In both toys, the four commuting equations are direct consequences of the displayed transitions; `q_c(v,m,n)=(v,m)` and `q_d(v,h)=(v,parity(h))` each attain all four reduced states. No action or state is silently omitted.

**Coarsest refinement.** For either toy, write `d(w)` for visible output decoded from a competing exact refinement `r`, and `G_probe` for its reduced probe transition. Compatibility yields
`d(G_probe(r(x))) = d(r(T_probe(x))) = p(T_probe(x))`; the first coordinate is `d(r(x))=p(x)`. In these particular toys `p(T_probe(x))=m(x)`, so `H(w)=(d(w),d(G_probe(w)))` satisfies `H∘r=q`. The bridge separately proves that `q` itself is exact. Thus it is coarsest **among exact refinements retaining visible output for this declared four-action system**. Since `q` is onto four values, any such `r` has at least four observed states. This argument is a partition/factorization result, not an entropy, energy, biological minimum, or universal abstraction cost. If probe is removed, `p=v` is exact; if the probe rule changes to depend on nuisance/last-symbol detail, the claimed quotient can fail.

**Unbounded decision records and trajectories.** The finite script checks only initial decision histories of length at most 3 and action words of length at most 4. The written identities `parity(ε)=0`, `parity(h0)=parity(h)`, and `parity(h1)=1−parity(h)` hold for arbitrary finite words, so they establish all-state actionwise compatibility independently of those bounds. `ExactAbstraction.trajectory_preservation` then uses induction for every natural horizon under the **same** input sequence. Equal current visible output alone, or different controllers choosing different actions, gives no such guarantee. The worked trace is consistent with the transition table.
