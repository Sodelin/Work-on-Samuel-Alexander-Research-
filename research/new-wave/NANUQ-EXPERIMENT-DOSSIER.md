# NANUQ experiment: what the target changes actually bought

Prepared 29 September 2026 Pacific / 30 September UTC. This is a retrospective of the recorded experiment and a proposal for testing a better research method. It is not a new mathematical proof, a token-cost measurement or an explanation of the model's hidden motivations.

## Finding

The strongest supported diagnosis is that **target formulation and representation choice can dominate incremental proof work**. The successful all-level move replaces a growing classification of network cores with a quantity whose dependence is bounded independently of network level. Later sharpness and transfer results reuse that machinery. Both premature narrowing and useful investment in foundations are visible; the record does not quantify their relative cost.

The first New Wave protocol had the same risk: it made subtasks answer to a registered question without independently challenging whether that question was the right one. [Revision 2](https://github.com/Sodelin/Formalizing-Soft-Sciences/blob/main/NEW-WAVE.md) separates target discovery from execution. A target challenge can replace the question, rather than merely add a strengthening to it.

## Why the all-level change matters

The earlier approach asks which cores occur at level two or three, then enumerates their decorations. Raising the level expands the classification obligation. The successful alternative asks what information a circularity coefficient actually consumes.

For fixed anchors p,q, a coefficient compares four matrix entries involving boundary labels a,b,c,d. Its total dependence is contained in {p,q,a,b,c,d}: at most six distinct taxa. The paired-tip representation permits deleting every unused label, including both occurrences of every unnecessary hybrid taxon. Tree restriction preserves the queried quartet sets and boundary adjacencies. A negative coefficient at any finite level would therefore appear in the finite certificate.

This establishes an unbounded conclusion through a proved finite dependence, rather than induction over increasingly complicated levels or extrapolation from passing examples. The retained source assumptions—binary, semi-directed LSA, outer-labeled planar and galled—remain hypotheses of the stated theorem. All levels does not mean all networks.

The finite certificate represents 84,076 ordered tree/duplication instances, 122 distinct quartet systems and 24,667 anchor coefficients. Those numbers are coverage evidence for the reduced domain, not counts of discoveries. The structural proof supplies the bridge from the unbounded class. Complete source-class Lean formalization remains incomplete in the publication record.

The five-cutoff result is a different insight. Equality of normalized inequality catalogs means that six-label tests impose no parameter condition beyond those already witnessed with five labels. It does not mean that each six-label configuration can be pruned to a matching five-label configuration. This sharpening reused the existing certificate and obstruction; its note records no new enumeration or Lean build. That is genuine evidence of low marginal verification work, without establishing token savings.

## Ease, usage and motivations

Three explanations fit the observed trajectory: earlier work supplied reusable infrastructure; the initial target was unnecessarily narrow; and the new representation bypassed a growing classification burden. They can all be true. The record supports reuse of anchors, exact computation and composition, and explicitly documents a user-directed shift away from raw-graph expansion. It does not show that the final result could have been obtained from an empty starting point at the same marginal cost.

The reported change in account usage is not a per-task measurement. No defensible ratio compares the foundational work with the all-level extension, and no quantified speedup is claimed. Commit intervals measure publication timing, not continuous reasoning time. Measuring savings requires actual token/time data and matched comparisons.

Completion-oriented training or narrow prompting might contribute to target fixation. The observable hypothesis is that the assistant too often accepts a readily deliverable formulation before challenging its restrictions and surrounding system. GitHub cannot identify the hidden causal mechanism. Reading repository material changes available context, not model weights; an inference-time feeling of reward or dopamine is not established. We can improve the behavior without proving a motivational theory.

The Wong comparison reinforces a distinct scope risk. The [bridge status](../../notes/WONG-ALEXANDER-BRIDGE-STATUS.md) distinguishes finite record reconstruction from infinite population/species claims and notes that faithful established-result formalization is not new open-problem closure. A large body of representation code can be useful yet fail to address the desired bridge. This dossier does not reconstruct all Wong or Collatz sessions and therefore does not label their work globally unnecessary or assign costs to them.

## The shadow idea: useful formulation and limit

Interpret illumination as understanding the mechanisms and dependencies needed for an outcome, and shadow as the observable structure preserved under a projection or transfer. This vocabulary usefully directs attention to both internal coverage and external applicability. It does not itself supply a theorem about topology or establish universal self-similarity.

For an observation map O from hidden states to observations and a desired quantity f, exact recovery requires f to agree on every fibre of O. If two admissible states have the same observation but different f, no decoder can recover f from that observation alone. Thus similar shadows need not identify their sources. The repository's [observation framework](../../lean/SamuelAlexanderResearch/ObservationPrediction.lean) and [predictive-state work](../open-problems/time-self-reference/predictive-memory/PredictiveState.lean) provide precise related statements; this dossier makes no new build claim.

For psychology, the productive next question is which measurements and interventions distinguish otherwise observationally equivalent mechanisms, and which conclusions remain invariant without that distinction. For NANUQ, the multicopy transfer preserves quartet sets/support but loses some global tree choices and probabilities. That explicit preserved/lost-information distinction makes the transfer reliable.

## Next-use boundary

The [external extension map](../nanuq-all-level-2026-09-29/EXPLICIT-EXTENSION-TARGETS.md) proposes fewer quartet queries, higher-level partial reconstruction, quarnet encoding and other targets. It states that no additional external problem is already closed by analogy. For sparse anchor sampling, nonnegative summands alone do not preserve support: every informative split needs a selected positive witness. A useful sampling theorem needs uniform coverage, query cost and the data/inference bridge. The target challenge must discover such missing mechanisms rather than market a conditional algebraic observation as a completed algorithm.

## Test the revised method

NANUQ is a retrospective case, not a clean benchmark: its known answer contaminates replay. Use several held-out project snapshots with the same sources, starting artifacts and total resource budgets. Compare the first protocol with revision 2, using multiple independent runs and blinded outcome review where feasible.

Record actual model/effort, tools, time and tokens when observable; unknown otherwise. Separate discovery, execution, verification and documentation costs. Judge source correspondence, central-outcome coverage, strongest correct statement, retained assumptions, verified transfer and unresolved gaps. Include failed scope challenges, misleading similarities and tasks whose existing target was already appropriate.

Predeclare the main comparison: meaningful verified scope/outcome improvement within equal budgets, plus total cost when measured. Report failures and uncertainty, not just the best run. Do not infer greater efficiency from more general wording, more agents or more paperwork. A successful test would show that the bounded target challenge improves results without an offsetting resource penalty; it would not prove a global optimal strategy or identify an internal reward mechanism.
