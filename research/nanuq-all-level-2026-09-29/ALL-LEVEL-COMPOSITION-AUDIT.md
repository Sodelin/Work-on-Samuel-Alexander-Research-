# All-level composition audit for the original NANUQ distance

Date: 29 September 2026.

**Verdict: accepted.** The exact composition identity in `COMPOSITION-PROOF.md`, Sections 1-3, holds at every finite reticulation level for binary semi-directed LSA, outer-labeled planar, galled networks. None of its steps requires level at most two. The level restriction enters the old proof through local positivity, not through composition. The all-level local anchor result can therefore be substituted into this identity to obtain circular decomposability for networks with multiple blobs in the same class.

This verdict concerns the **original** NANUQ distance, which uses the uniform average over distinct displayed quartet topologies. It does not review a generalized parameter-family composition identity or prove equality of metric support with the displayed-split system. It is a symbolic mathematical audit, not a Lean certificate or a new execution of the finite checker.

## 1. Reviewed scope and identity

I read `ALL-LEVEL-PROOF.md`, Section 7; `ordinal/COMPOSITION-PROOF.md`, its statement and Sections 1-3; and `source/FINAL-SOURCE-REVIEW.md`, Section 2, for the explicit local-root construction. The composition argument reviewed here is reproduced with its missing level qualifier removed and its needed hypotheses justified below.

Let N be a finite network in the stated class, on X with n=|X|>=4. For distinct x,y, the original distance is

    d_N(x,y) = 2 sum_{{z,w} subset X minus {x,y}} rho_N,{x,y,z,w}(x,y) + 2n-4,

where all pair sums are unordered and rho is the average of the cherry-separation indicator over the distinct displayed quartet topologies. Set d_N(x,x)=0.

Include every non-leaf blob B in the composition, including a singleton trivalent tree vertex. Let L_B be the blob capped at its incident cut edges with distinct pendant port labels P_B. Let X_B(a) be the taxon set attached at port a, m_B(a)=|X_B(a)|, and pi_B the map sending a global taxon to its port.

For a local network L on ports P and masses m, define the zero-diagonal matrix

    W_L(m)(a,b) =
      2 sum_{{c,d} subset P minus {a,b}} m(c)m(d) rho_L,{a,b,c,d}(a,b)
      + (m(a)+m(b)) sum_{c in P minus {a,b}} m(c),    a != b.

The identity accepted here is

    d_N(x,y) = sum_{B non-leaf blob} W_{L_B}(m_B)(pi_B(x),pi_B(y)).      (C)

The source definitions are Holtgrefe et al., DOI [10.1007/s11538-025-01549-4](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11538-025-01549-4), Definitions 2.1-2.5, the displayed-tree definition following Definition 2.5, Lemma 3.2, Equation (10), and Definition 4.1. These definitions and the displayed-quartet restriction identity are not limited to level two. Allman et al., DOI [10.1007/s11538-025-01545-8](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11538-025-01545-8), Section 3 Definition 6, also describes the bloblet generated by a blob. The arguments below justify the actual binary LSA admission needed here.

## 2. Local capping is admissible without a level bound

### 2.1 Positive port masses and partition of X

Every cut edge separates taxa on both sides. To verify this, work in a rooted LSA partner. A descendant-side component contains a leaf, by following directed edges in the finite acyclic graph. If a rootward component contained no taxon, all root-to-taxon paths would pass through the child endpoint of the cut edge. That endpoint would be a stable ancestor of all taxa strictly below the root, contradicting the LSA condition. If the suppressed root lies on the cut edge itself, the two directed branches from the root each lead to a taxon.

A non-leaf blob contains no taxon leaf: a degree-one taxon is its own trivial blob. Therefore the components attached at B's ports partition X, with every part nonempty. This proves that m_B is a positive integer-valued mass function and pi_B is defined on all of X. It also rules out a non-leaf terminal blob in the blob tree, so every included blob has at least two ports.

### 2.2 Degrees, hybrid edges, and galledness

In this galled class each hybrid incoming edge belongs to the cycle witnessing that hybrid's gall. It is consequently not a cut edge. Both incoming edges of a hybrid lie in one blob, so every hybrid choice belongs entirely to a single blob.

Capping cut edges changes neither vertex degrees within B nor any hybrid direction. New cap vertices are labeled leaves. A singleton non-leaf blob is an ordinary degree-three tree vertex and caps to a three-leaf star. For a nontrivial blob, every witnessing gall is entirely inside B, because a cycle cannot traverse a cut edge. It survives capping. Thus the capped local graph is binary and galled, regardless of the number of hybrids in B.

### 2.3 A rooted LSA partner really exists

The partly directed graph must have a rooted LSA partner; degree checks alone would not establish the source hypothesis.

If the root of a chosen rooted partner lies in B, retain its orientation on B and replace each outgoing attachment by its port leaf. A strict descendant of the root that dominated all port leaves would dominate all original taxa, contradicting the original LSA property. Thus the retained root remains the LSA of the local ports.

If the root lies outside B, the unique rootward cut edge enters B at a tree node, since it is not hybrid. Subdivide the rootward port's pendant edge by a new root. Direct one root edge to that port leaf and the other into B; keep the original orientations in B and towards the other ports. This is acyclic and has the required binary indegrees and outdegrees. Its new root is the LSA: the rootward port is its direct leaf child, while every other port is below its other child. The same construction handles a root suppressed on a bridge.

This establishes local LSA admissibility even though a rootward component may contain an arbitrarily large or reticulate subnetwork before capping.

### 2.4 Outer-labeled planarity and small port counts

Retain the original planar embedding of B. A port attachment cannot lie inside a bounded face of B: its attached component has a taxon, and, with only that one attachment to B, it could not reach the global outer face without crossing B. All ports can therefore be capped in outer-face corners. L_B is an outer-labeled planar galled bloblet.

For at least four ports, it lies in exactly the arbitrary-level bloblet class to which the local theorem applies. Two- and three-port cases need no four-leaf theorem. With two ports W is identically zero. With three ports a,b,c every displayed tree is the unique three-leaf star, and

    W(a,b) = (m(a)+m(b))m(c).

Its pendant lengths are m(b)m(c), m(a)m(c), and m(a)m(b). These formulas cover every local reticulation presentation with those port counts. No low-level classification is being substituted for the general case.

## 3. The switching-weighted identity is level-independent

### 3.1 Weighted tree identity

For a binary tree T with terminal masses m, give each incident edge at an internal vertex the endpoint contribution equal to the product of the masses in the other two branches. Add the two endpoint contributions for an internal edge; a leaf endpoint contributes zero.

The resulting path distance D_T(m) equals W_T(m). This is verified monomial by monomial for the distance between terminals a,b:

- m(a)m(b) has coefficient zero.
- If exactly one of c,d belongs to {a,b}, the coefficient of m(c)m(d) is one, contributed at the branching point of the three-terminal subtree.
- If a,b,c,d are distinct, that coefficient is zero when a,b form a cherry in the restricted quartet, and two otherwise, contributed at the two branching points on the a-b path.

These are exactly the coefficients in the definition of W_T. Every product involves disjoint branches, so squared-mass terms do not appear. This includes the explicit two- and three-terminal conventions above.

### 3.2 Global and local switchings are a product

Fix a rooted partner and choose one retained incoming edge independently at every hybrid. After recursive pruning of unlabeled leaves and degree-two suppression, let T_sigma be the global displayed tree. Every original cut edge separates nonempty taxon sets, so it is retained in the connecting tree, possibly as part of a suppressed path.

Because both incoming edges of every hybrid lie in one blob, restricting sigma to B yields the usual local switching sigma_B of L_B. Conversely, arbitrary local choices in all blobs extend to a global switching. Capping does not impose an extra compatibility constraint. Thus the global choice set is the Cartesian product of the local choice sets, at arbitrary level.

### 3.3 Group endpoint contributions by blob

Every degree-three branching vertex of T_sigma is an original tree vertex: deletion and suppression create no new branch vertices. It belongs to one unique original blob B. Its three global taxon branches are unions of whole B-port components, so their sizes are exactly the sums of the masses m_B on the corresponding branches of the local displayed tree T_B,sigma_B.

Conversely, a branching vertex of that local displayed tree remains a global branching vertex because all its port masses are positive. If the x-y path enters B, its portion corresponds to the path between pi_B(x) and pi_B(y). If both taxa occupy the same B-port component, their global path does not enter B and the local distance is zero.

Grouping endpoint contributions consequently proves, separately for every switching,

    d_T_sigma(x,y) = sum_B D_T_B,sigma_B(m_B)(pi_B(x),pi_B(y)).         (T)

The sum must include singleton trivalent blobs. Two-port blobs contribute zero because no local branching vertex remains; degree-two chains do not create missing endpoint terms.

Average (T) over uniformly chosen hybrid-edge switchings. The Cartesian-product observation makes each local marginal uniform, although different displayed topologies may have different multiplicities. This gives an exact identity for the switching-weighted quartet metric. No bound on the number of switchings is required for the symbolic averaging argument.

## 4. Distinct-topology corrections localize exactly

Write rho_sw,N,Q for the expected cherry-separation indicator of the restriction of T_sigma to Q, under uniform hybrid-edge choices. The original metric differs from the averaged tree metric by

    E_N(x,y) = 2 sum_{{z,w} subset X minus {x,y}}
                        (rho_N,Q(x,y) - rho_sw,N,Q(x,y)),

where Q={x,y,z,w}. The constant 2n-4 cancels. The source's Lemma 3.2 gives the correspondence with the switching distribution on the induced quarnet; its positive-multiplicity identity also ensures equality of the displayed-quartet supports under restriction.

Contract the blobs of N to obtain its tree of blobs, retaining the global taxon leaves. Restrict this tree to Q and suppress degree-two vertices. Exactly two cases are possible.

**Resolved quartet.** A cut edge separates Q two versus two. It survives every switching, so every global displayed tree has the same restricted quartet topology. Both the uniform-distinct and switching averages are the same point mass. The discrepancy is zero.

**Four-leaf star.** There is a unique central vertex of the restricted blob tree, corresponding to a unique blob B. The four taxa occupy four distinct ports of B. In any global switching, all portions outside B reduce to pendant paths on restriction to Q, so the quartet topology is exactly the restriction of T_B,sigma_B to those four ports. Every local switching extends globally.

These statements prove two different facts, both needed:

1. The global and central-local **sets of distinct quartet topologies** agree, so their uniform-distinct rho values agree.
2. The uniform global switching distribution has the uniform local switching distribution as its marginal, so their **switching-average** rho values agree.

It is not necessary, and would be incorrect in general, to claim equal multiplicities for the distinct topologies. Outside choices merely repeat each fixed local switching equally often.

Thus every nonzero discrepancy is assigned to one and only one central blob. For x,y in distinct ports a,b of B, choosing the other two taxa from distinct other ports c,d gives exactly m_B(c)m_B(d) unordered pairs. No other occupancy pattern contributes a discrepancy assigned to B. Hence

    E_N(x,y) = sum_B E_B(m_B)(pi_B(x),pi_B(y)),

where, for a != b,

    E_B(m)(a,b) = 2 sum_{{c,d} subset P_B minus {a,b}} m(c)m(d)
        (rho_L_B,{a,b,c,d}(a,b) - rho_sw,L_B,{a,b,c,d}(a,b)),

and E_B(a,a)=0.

Adding this exact discrepancy identity to averaged (T), and using the weighted tree identity, produces (C). Every level restriction has disappeared from the proof because none was used. This proves composition for the original NANUQ distance, not just for the auxiliary switching-weighted one.

## 5. All lifted local decompositions share one circular order

Fix a single outer-labeled planar embedding of the full N and its cyclic leaf order C_X. For any blob B, traversing the outer face crosses an incident bridge into its attached component and eventually returns along that same bridge. All taxon leaves of that component occur in the resulting single contour interval. Thus each X_B(a) is a contiguous block in C_X.

Collapsing those blocks gives the circular port order C_B induced by the inherited embedding of L_B. If A|P_B-minus-A is circular in C_B, the lifted taxon split

    union_{a in A} X_B(a)  |  union_{a outside A} X_B(a)

is circular in C_X: a consecutive set of port blocks is a consecutive set of global taxa. Its split metric is exactly the pullback of the local split metric by pi_B.

Therefore local nonnegative circular decompositions in all blobs, including the explicit three-port star and zero two-port cases, lift to nonnegative decompositions in the **same** C_X. Applying the already audited all-level anchor-positivity result to the positive masses m_B supplies those local decompositions. Equation (C) then makes the full original NANUQ distance circular decomposable. The lower bound d_N(x,y)>=2n-4>0 for distinct x,y gives separation; the split decomposition gives the triangle inequality.

This conclusion is for the finite binary LSA, outer-labeled planar, galled class at arbitrary level. It does not remove those structural hypotheses.

## 6. Audit conclusion and limits

There is **no unresolved mathematical gap in this narrow transfer**: positive port masses, local rooted LSA admission, preservation of galledness and outer-face placement, local/global switching support and marginal distributions, discrepancy localization, and a common circular order all have level-independent arguments above.

The old composition proof's level-at-most-two assumption may be removed from the identity, while retaining the rest of its hypotheses. Substituting the separately accepted all-level local positivity theorem then proves circularity for the full multi-blob class, beyond the bloblet conclusion in the integration proof's initial statement.

This audit does not establish an all-level canonical form, a global support equality, any parameter-family transfer, statistical inference guarantees, or historical novelty. It does not rerun or re-audit the finite local certificate. The final circularity theorem still inherits the verification level of that exact computer-assisted local result. These are scope boundaries, not an unproved step in identity (C).
