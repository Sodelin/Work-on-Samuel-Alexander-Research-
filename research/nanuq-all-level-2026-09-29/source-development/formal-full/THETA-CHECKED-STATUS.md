# Canonical theta lane: checked status

This lane now proves nonnegative circular anchor coefficients for canonical theta components with arbitrary natural arm lengths. It also proves that the executable tensor equals the source mean over distinct displayed quartet topologies on every four distinct valid taxa. The passage from an arbitrary source-network graph to canonical components and its global composition are separate obligations owned by the parent lane.

The strongest assembled arbitrary-arm theorem is `Nanuq.Theta.unbounded_source_theorem` in `AnchorUnboundedTheorem.lean` (ordinal lane). It gives circular decomposability, pseudometric laws, and strict off-diagonal positivity for the source metric when `1 ≤ t.total`. The mass-weighted circular theorem permits every nonnegative rational mass; exact agreement between weighted and unit-mass positive supports requires every mass strictly positive. There is no upper bound on an original arm length or original leaf count.

The independently compiled semantics endpoint is `Nanuq.Theta.rhoFin_source` in `ThetaSourceUnbounded.lean`. Its only substantive leaf hypotheses are six pairwise inequalities among the four valid finite indices. `quartet_resolves` proves that the executable Option fallback is never used on those inputs.

The proof chain is concrete:

1. `ThetaFinite.lean` constructs all four switching trees by their root-to-terminal paths, computes their path distances, resolves quartets by strict four-point comparisons, removes duplicate topologies, and averages over that distinct set. It does not average switching multiplicities.
2. `ThetaCertificate.lean` checks all 209 four-arm count tuples with ordinary total 1 through 6 and every one of their 100823 anchor coefficients. `ThetaSupportCertificate.lean` also checks equality of union-of-positive-anchor support and union-of-displayed-edge support for those same templates. These are proof-producing kernel reductions (`decide +kernel`) of the actual evaluator. No precomputed quartet, metric, sign, or support answers are used as theorem assumptions. `ZeroTheta.lean`, written by the ordinal lane, additionally checks the degenerate zero-ordinary-leaf case for positivity only.
3. `ThetaArmMetric.lean` proves an unbounded arithmetic characterization of a strict four-point minimum in a four-arm H-tree: it is an arm-tail cut or the central cut. Only the four arm tags are case-split; attachment positions remain arbitrary natural variables.
4. `ThetaPathLengths.lean`, `ThetaBumpedMetric.lean`, and `ThetaQuartetOrder.lean` identify the actual path quartet with that arm/order criterion. Moving hybrid pendant attachment positions by one adds an endpoint-dependent constant to distances and does not change any four-point comparison. Validity puts a hybrid strictly after all ordinary leaves on its chosen arm.
5. `ThetaCompression.lean` proves that rank-compressing any retained finite set of valid leaves preserves each actual switched quartet, hence the exact deduplicated source average and all anchor entries with distinct anchors. The ordinal lane proves the selected six labels plus retained hybrids occupy at most six ordinary positions, preserves their cyclic adjacency and ordered indices, and invokes the finite certificate.
6. `ThetaPathMembership.lean` and `ThetaSwitchingEdges.lean` provide exact descendant predicates and an iff between real edge children and nonroot vertices occurring on valid terminal paths, for the remaining unbounded displayed-support proof.

Every listed successful theorem was compiled with the existing pinned Lean 4.33.1 runtime. Successful endpoint logs report only the standard axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound` (some arithmetic lemmas use fewer). There are no introduced axioms, `sorry`, `native_decide`, `Lean.ofReduceBool`, or unsafe proof shortcuts in the owned `Theta*.lean` modules.

The ordinal lane subsequently completed `AnchorUnboundedSupport.lean`: `unbounded_support_exact`, `unbounded_weighted_exact_support`, and `unbounded_source_full_theorem` now prove exact displayed-edge support for arbitrary original arm lengths (ordinary total at least one), in addition to circular decomposition, pseudometric laws, source semantics, and positive-mass support agreement. This conclusion uses the checked path/edge, circular-boundary, and witness-lifting bridges; it is not inferred solely from the finite table. A complete theorem for the paper's raw network class must additionally discharge the parent graph/composition bridges. This snapshot does not claim a full-class Lean theorem, an independent publication-priority certificate, or empirical biological identification.
