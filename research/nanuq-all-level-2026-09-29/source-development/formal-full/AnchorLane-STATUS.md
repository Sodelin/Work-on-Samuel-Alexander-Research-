# Weighted anchors, compression, and exact theta support

This lane now proves the complete local canonical-theta theorem for arbitrary natural arm lengths. The endpoint is `Nanuq.Theta.unbounded_source_full_theorem` in `AnchorUnboundedSupport.lean`, compiled with the pinned Lean 4.33.1 driver. Its printed axiom dependencies are only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`.

The theorem assumes `1 ≤ t.total`: there are at least three canonical leaves, including the two hybrid leaves. It concludes circular decomposability of the source NANUQ matrix, all pseudometric laws, strict positive distance between distinct taxa, and positivity of a circular coefficient exactly when the associated split is displayed by an actual edge in one of the four switching trees. `unbounded_weighted_exact_support` proves that same actual-edge support for arbitrary positive rational terminal masses. The source uses the uniform mean over distinct quartet resolutions; `ThetaSourceUnbounded.rhoFin_source` in the embedding lane proves the semantic correspondence without an arm-length bound.

The zero ordinary-leaf template is covered by anchor nonnegativity, circular decomposition, and pseudometric results, but is deliberately excluded from exact support: its two-taxon source matrix is zero even though a switching edge displays the two-taxon split.

The completed proof chain is:

1. `WeightedAnchor` proves the literal source weighted formula equals the sum of unordered anchor matrices with coefficients `m p * m q`; it includes the endpoint mass terms. `WeightedCoefficients` distributes circular coefficients through that sum and proves exact positive support for positive masses. `WeightedSource` connects unit masses to the source formula. `WeightedOrderedPairs` supplies the factor of two for the ordered-pair normalization used in the parent composition lane. `WeightedSmallPorts` treats two and three ports explicitly.
2. `AnchorThetaBridge` proves the executable theta evaluator and the weighted anchor definitions agree, including a proof that the canonical leaf list has no duplicates. The finite 209-template positivity and exact-support certificates are sibling-owned kernel-checked proofs, not external Python assumptions.
3. `AnchorCompression`, `AnchorCanonicalOrder`, `AnchorIndexTransport`, and related files retain at most six ordinary witness leaves and both hybrid leaves. They prove order-preserving rank compression, literal equality of the filtered/compressed canonical list, ordered index transport, and preservation of cyclic successors, including wraparound. `AnchorMetricOrder` supplies the exact position-order interface used by the embedding lane to prove arbitrary-arm quartet and anchor preservation.
4. `AnchorUnboundedTheorem` discharges the compression premise using `ThetaCompression.anchor_compression`. It proves arbitrary-arm anchor nonnegativity and weighted circular decomposition. No unbounded positivity premise remains.
5. `AnchorLeafPosition` proves explicit canonical positions invert the actual list lookup. `CircularThetaCuts` proves every concrete vertex-descendant predicate is an interval of those positions or its complement. This includes empty/full predicates and does not assume circularity.
6. `CircularThetaSupport`, using the embedding lane's actual edge/quartet equivalence and displayed-split semantics, identifies a nontrivial full split from its four boundary leaves. Adjacent boundary cases are handled with actual terminal edges. `CircularSupportCompression` then proves the actual Boolean `displayedSplit` predicate is preserved in both directions by boundary-preserving compression.
7. `AnchorUnboundedSupport` transports positive finite anchor witnesses back by the embedding lane's proved compressed-index surjectivity and order reflection. In the other direction it transports a positive original anchor to a bounded template. `AnchorNondegenerate` prevents the retained witnesses of an original theta with at least three leaves from collapsing to the unsupported two-leaf case.

The local exact-support gap is now closed. This does not by itself prove that every network in the paper's general graph class has the formal representation, nor that arbitrary raw graph switchings satisfy the parent lane's global localization/composition identity. Those remain separate obligations. The local source theorem should not be reported as the completed B2-to-N2 conjecture until those bridges are supplied.
