Holtgrefe et al.'s January 2026 correction, DOI
[10.1007/s11538-025-01564-5](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11538-025-01564-5),
restores a figure and does not change the target theorem.

The distance theorem addresses an explicitly recorded mathematical inference
problem. Its practical value is an exact split representation compatible with
circular-distance methods at unbounded level. Turning that representation into
a statistically consistent biological inference procedure requires a data
model, a valid quartet estimator, and a proved output guarantee.

Existing all-level identification results and efficient level-one algorithms
remain credited. In particular, Dai and Molloy's August 2026
[NetCS paper](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.WABI.2026.1) already establishes
fast, consistent level-one blob reconstruction under its assumptions. We do
not claim that achievement as a consequence newly discovered here.

The bounded literature pass establishes these exact connections. It is not an
exhaustive worldwide novelty or current-open-status certification. The detailed
search record and proposed different problems are in
`explicit-extension-search-ledger.json` and `EXPLICIT-EXTENSION-TARGETS.md`.
