# What “self-similarity” and “the bridge” mean here

**The checked results establish exact recursive structure in one particular
label sequence and its matching-height function. They do not establish that
Alexander's population graphs in general are self-similar, or identify them
with a separate biological or dynamical theory.** The intended “other
research” still needs to be identified before claiming a particular bridge.

This is a bounded scope audit of existing definitions and endpoints, not a
new theorem or research direction.

## The objects must stay distinct

[Alexander's 2013 population definition](https://arxiv.org/html/1212.0186v2#S1)
allows arbitrary infinite directed genealogical graphs satisfying finite
roots, finite children, strict birth order with finite birthdate sublevels,
and incoming coverage of every required label. It assumes no substitution
rule, scale map, graph isomorphism, or fractal structure. His positive theorem
concerns eventually periodic words in every eligible population.

The separate [September 2026 classification manuscript, Section 2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/avg-netizen/biological-unavoidability/3d6175e3e23f67bd68e7be591b5a9a6d04e496a3/paper.md)
constructs a particular target-dependent population `P_s`. Its vertices are
natural numbers. Every target `w≥2` receives edges from `w−1` and `w−2`, with
complementary labels determined by
`row(s,2j)=s(j)` and `row(s,2j+1)=1−s(j)`. The edge `0→1` is absent.
This construction should not be attributed to Alexander's 2013 paper.

`BinaryPopulation.forget_edge_eq` proves that **all these constructed `P_s`
have exactly the same unlabelled graph**. Their different target words change
the edge labels. Equality of these unlabelled graphs is a different statement
from an isomorphism between a graph and a smaller or rescaled copy of itself.

## Exact recursive statements already checked

| Object | Checked content | Main endpoints |
|---|---|---|
| The actual Thue–Morse bits `t` | `t(2n)=t(n)` and `t(2n+1)=1−t(n)`. These are the precise binary recurrences behind the usual substitution description. | `ThueMorseBits.t_double`, `t_double_add_one` |
| Dyadic color blocks | For `b<2^r`, `t(a·2^r+b)=t(a) xor t(b)`. | `ThueMorseBits.t_dyadic_block` |
| Finite boundary trajectories | Exact blocks of advances by one or two, dyadic ascent/descent, and translation inside explicitly bounded windows where the colors agree. These are auxiliary frontier boundaries, not automatically matching paths. | `SharpThueMorse.descent_block`, `common_descent`; `FullHeight.window_translate`, `ascent_step` |
| Particular finite extremal paths | A recursively constructed dyadic family of actual matching paths, with checked head, staircase and translated half-tail conditions. | `PhaseExtremal.half_translate`, `extremal_path` |
| Actual maximum matching length `L(v)` | A closed form at every natural start, including zero, plus ten integer coordinates updated by reading a binary digit. The evaluator returns an attained actual graph maximum. | `FullHeight.height_closed_form`; `DigitRecurrence.actual_digit_recurrence`, `evaluate_actual_height`, `evaluate_isMaximum` |

The supporting files are [ThueMorseBits](../lean/SamuelAlexanderResearch/ThueMorseBits.lean),
[SharpThueMorse](../lean/SamuelAlexanderResearch/SharpThueMorse.lean),
[FullHeight](../lean/SamuelAlexanderResearch/FullHeight.lean),
[PhaseExtremal](../lean/SamuelAlexanderResearch/PhaseExtremal.lean), and
[DigitRecurrence](../lean/SamuelAlexanderResearch/DigitRecurrence.lean).

The ten coordinates are integer-valued and can grow without bound. A fixed
number of coordinates is not a finite set of possible states or a claim that
the height sequence is a bounded automatic word. The optional
[`DigitRegularity.lean`](../real/DigitRegularity.lean) states the further
integer-module theorem `height_two_regular`: the module generated by all
subsequences `n ↦ L(2^e n+r)`, `r<2^e`, is finitely generated. At this audit,
the checked formula and executable recurrence were complete, while the
[current handoff](../HANDOFF-FOR-ALEXANDER.md) still marked this separate
Mathlib wrapper's compilation as pending. That build status must be updated
from its actual check, not inferred from the presence of theorem source.

No literal graph self-similarity isomorphism was found among the exported
core or optional-real endpoints. In particular, the bit identities alone
do not make vertex doubling an edge-preserving map: doubling a permitted
advance of two would give an advance of four, outside `P_s`'s edge definition.
This is a scope observation from the definitions, not an additional Lean
isomorphism or non-isomorphism theorem.

## Which bridge could the user mean?

**Between the word-avoidance construction and Alexander's species papers:**
there is a proved, concrete bridge. `BinaryPopulation.explicit_specieslike_avoider`
checks that the constructed binary avoiding graph is an eligible population
whose whole unlabelled graph is maximal specieslike and reflecting, while
lacking a common ancestor. `PositiveUnavoidability.specieslike_classification`
gives the binary classification inside the specieslike restriction. These
results connect precisely stated graph predicates; they do not classify
empirical species. The ancestry observations have precedents in Alexander's
2013 inspecies results and 2026 Example 14(1), as recorded in the
[prior-work audit](../PRIOR-WORK-AUDIT.md).

**Between population paths and automatic/regular-sequence mathematics:** the
actual-height formula and binary digit recurrence are a proved bridge for
this specific Thue–Morse population. This is the appropriate place to use
“recursive” or “dyadic structure.” It is not a theorem about every avoiding
population or every substitution sequence.

**Between Alexander and Levin, Friston, morphogenesis, or another dynamical
model:** the repository's [complex-systems interface](../explorations/COMPLEX-SYSTEMS-INTERFACE.md)
explicitly remains exploratory. No theorem instantiates a particular such
model, defines its state-to-lineage map, verifies the population axioms for
that map, and transfers the desired scientific conclusion. Generic ancestry,
projection or observation lemmas do not supply that missing model by
themselves. The synthetic stateful-CA example is a checked method example,
not a formalization of one of those published biological models.

## Reading the current repository accurately

At this audit, [RESEARCH-MAP.md](../RESEARCH-MAP.md) and
[QUESTION-LEDGER.md](../QUESTION-LEDGER.md) still described the full-height
formula and several other now-checked branches as open. Those passages lag
the current endpoints and [TEN-SOLUTIONS.md](../TEN-SOLUTIONS.md); they should
not be used as the present theorem inventory. This audit did not edit those
shared documents.

Lean evidence, mathematical interpretation, an email draft, a VibeMath entry,
and an online publication are separate artifacts. A checked result can
support the mathematical statements in each, but does not itself identify
which broader research connection the user intends or authorize a publication
claim. This note makes no outreach or publication change.
