# Answers to the ten research proposals

This work continues [the original ten proposals](TEN-RESEARCH-IDEAS.md) from merged baseline `425fee8c90e15656caea954ac92beaf28a5d869f`. It records exact mathematical answers and checks, including counterexamples when an unrestricted proposed extension fails. Literature priority is distinct from proof status.

The integrated core audit checks 370 selected endpoints in 47 Std-only modules. The actual population encoder and literal real CA comparison have also passed individual Lean checks and independent statement review; their combined Mathlib receipt is being refreshed. Existing quantitative theorems retain their original attribution and verification history. The [source comparison](notes/source-audits/PROPOSALS3-10-SOURCE-AUDIT.md) records established methods and search limits.

Further refinements remain visible in the original proposals: joint phase digit recurrences, the best smaller additive constant for each individual edited target, optimal root counts, classifications beyond minimum crossing width, and gains for natural binary or published CA rules. The sharp universal finite-edit bound and its equality set are proved; an individual target can still admit a smaller allowance. Resolving a proposal's main question does not silently resolve all subsequent questions.

The productive-population adapters are binary and naturally indexed; the arbitrary-$`k`$ counting and rigidity statements have their stated broader scope. Repair optimality fixes the retained vertex set and permits edge deletion. The CA is synthetic and compares two precise classes of certificates for the same rule. None of these scope boundaries is an empirical biological classification or a claim about what Alexander has considered privately.
