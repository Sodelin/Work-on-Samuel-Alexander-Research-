## 1. Wong: count histories now have elapsed time

The earlier 19-declaration probability addition established a stopped count-path law and almost-sure arrival at one lineage after finitely many jumps. The new **24 selected declarations** attach independent exponential clocks at the specified rates and establish their conditional joint distribution.

Every positive initial count reaches one in finite physical time almost surely. Starting at one has time zero; starting at two or more has positive, finite time. All pre-absorption waits are positive and actual event times strictly increase.

[Readable proof guide](https://github.com/Sodelin/Work-on-Samuel-Alexander-Research-/blob/codex/research-followup-2026-09-25/research/wong/completion/probability/holding-times/README.md) · [Exact source](https://github.com/Sodelin/Work-on-Samuel-Alexander-Research-/blob/a2644182b147eb40816abb278e00c3c6d4709eb8/real/WongWaitingTimes.lean) · [Independent internal statement review](https://github.com/Sodelin/Work-on-Samuel-Alexander-Research-/blob/a2644182b147eb40816abb278e00c3c6d4709eb8/research/wong/completion/probability/holding-times/INDEPENDENT-REVIEW.json).

## 3. Information lost through simplification

The published controlled-abstraction bridge compares a finite cell-state toy and a decision device with arbitrarily long finite histories. Both admit the same four-state description for the chosen actions and observable.

A description retaining only the visible bit fails when a particular probe action is allowed. Adding one memory bit restores exact prediction. Its minimality is relative to those specified actions and that output.

[Worked example](https://github.com/Sodelin/Work-on-Samuel-Alexander-Research-/blob/codex/research-followup-2026-09-25/research/open-problems/time-self-reference/controlled-abstraction-bridge/BRIDGE.md) · [Evidence and reproduction](https://github.com/Sodelin/Work-on-Samuel-Alexander-Research-/blob/codex/research-followup-2026-09-25/research/open-problems/time-self-reference/controlled-abstraction-bridge/README.md). Evidence consists of written derivations, finite replay and independent internal review. Existing general Lean abstraction results are reused; the new example/refinement proof is not newly mechanized.
