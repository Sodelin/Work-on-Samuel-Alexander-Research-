# Refinements retained after the main ten-proposal results

The main solution ledger and the original proposals are distinct from these
stronger follow-up targets. None of the rows below is marked solved merely
because a related weaker theorem is available.

| Refinement | Existing result | Exact remaining objective and status |
|---|---|---|
| Joint phase and starting-index digit recurrence | Exact phase/start maxima and a phase-zero finite digit recurrence. | A finite recurrence or closed form treating both variables jointly, with its precise domain and any complexity claim. Open. |
| Individual finite-edit optimum | Sharp universal additive allowance, per-start exact maxima and equality tests. | A certified finite algorithm for the smallest integer allowance of each fixed finitely edited target. A concrete proposed reduction depends on the unproved branch-separation lemma in the linked note. Open. |
| Optimal root count at child cap two | A fixed-gender avoiding construction with three roots and optimal child cap. | Prove that three roots are necessary under the same requirements, or construct a two-root population meeting all of them. Open. |
| Crossing widths above the minimum | Exact minimum-width rigidity and finite-port encoding. | A structural and label-language classification at larger widths, including the binary width-four regime. Encoding alone does not settle it. Open. |
| Natural or published cellular automata | A complete synthetic three-state example separating the specified certificate classes. | A verified stronger bound for an actual natural binary or published rule, with the exact dynamics and meaningful prior-bound comparison. Open. |
| Literal binary-kernel finite generation | Checked finite-coordinate recurrence and digit evaluator. | The optional DigitRegularity wrapper remains outside the audited import graph until it is compiled and included in the manifest. Not certified by the current packet. |

The [individual finite-edit investigation](notes/REFINEMENT-INDIVIDUAL-FINITE-EDIT.md)
states a concrete candidate finite global search and its missing lemma. Its
exploratory numerical checks are not a proof, and the candidate is not advertised
as an algorithm already verified for all edits.

The [Wong source coverage map](notes/WONG-ALEXANDER-BRIDGE-STATUS.md) separately
retains stochastic models, full software serialization/simplification,
normalized interval output, and a justified biological owner/pedigree instance.
The checked infinite-completion theorem proves a limitation of finite data; it
does not supply the missing biological assumptions.
