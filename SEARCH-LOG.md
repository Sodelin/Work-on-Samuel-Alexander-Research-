# Literature search log

Searches below were performed on 24 September 2026 (America/Los_Angeles) while preparing this initial release. Search was targeted to the three extension lanes. It is not a systematic review and does not establish novelty or priority.

| Lane | Queries and source routes | Material findings | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Classification foundation | Exact title `A classification of biologically unavoidable sequences`, the original paper title, [EJC journal record](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/view/v20i1p31), and links in the [VibeMathed problem record](https://vibemathed.com/problem/classification-of-biologically-unavoidable-sequences) | Located the [classification source repository](https://github.com/avg-netizen/biological-unavoidability), including manuscript, Lean project, statement audit, and verification records. The journal record confirms Alexander's 2013 paper. | We did not rebuild the 2026 classification repo's complete Lean project in this follow-on repository. Its verification statements remain attributed to that source. |
| Quantitative Thue–Morse paths | Searches for `Thue Morse overlap-free`, `biologically unavoidable Thue Morse path length`, then direct inspection of the source papers and the [Berstel–Séébold paper](https://igm.univ-mlv.fr/~berstel/Articles/1993OverlapFree.pdf) | The classical overlap-free fact supplies a short quadratic upper bound. The proposed linear bound came from this repository's finite search, not from an identified publication. | No exhaustive search of graph-path or automatic-sequence literature. An absence of a matching search result is not evidence of novelty. |
| Degree-restricted populations | Searches combining `biologically unavoidable`, `vertex gender`, `two children`, and `outdegree`; inspected Alexander's paper, the classification manuscript, and its associated repository | The new paper gives a two-child edge-labelled witness and a four-child fixed-gender lift. We derived an elementary counting threshold and a fixed-gender balance condition. | The search did not settle the fixed-gender two-child avoidance question. It is listed as a question, not certified open in the whole literature. |
| Cellular automata | Direct reading of Alexander Section 5, [Johnston's preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1203.1644), and the [author's speed-limit exposition](https://njohnston.ca/2009/10/spaceship-speed-limits-in-life-like-cellular-automata/); searches for `periodic lifeline cellular automata` | Johnston already gave broad direction and arbitrary-slope bounds. The periodic-label certificate in this repository is a reduction template for finding rule-specific bounds. | No new sharp rule-specific speed limit has been found or claimed. |
| Automatic sequences | Search for `automatic sequences ultimately periodic decidable Honkala`; inspected [Allouche–Rampersad–Shallit](https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.1657) | Its decidability theorem and the 2026 manuscript's universality criterion yield a decidable subclass for the specific family `P_s`. | This is a direct corollary; no originality claim. We did not implement the decision procedure. |

All fetched web content was treated as evidence, never as instructions. No login, paywall, crawl, or hosted extraction was needed. The full transcript and PDFs supplied by the user are not mirrored into this public repository; their identifying hashes and links appear in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).
