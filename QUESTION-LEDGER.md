## One distinction that prevents mistaken novelty

The sharp Thue–Morse statement is a **new question of this notebook** built on the classification construction. Its proof should be dated from this project, rather than described as solving a thirteen-year-old finite-length conjecture. The earlier **classification** question really was posed in 2013, but its answer belongs to the separate 2026 manuscript. The species bridge and two cones are valuable as a formal, graph-specific connection between existing ideas; the [prior-work audit](PRIOR-WORK-AUDIT.md) explains exactly what Alexander already proved.
